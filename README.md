Statistical Learning Midterm Project

## Team

- Class: DS66B
- Group: 14
- Members: Trần Quang Huy, Bùi Ngọc Thuấn, Nguyễn Hoàng Thành

## Project

Topic 18: Electricity Consumption Prediction.

Working project name: Tetouan GridWatch. The initial goal is to forecast demand in three Tetouan distribution zones 30 minutes ahead and evaluate research-defined high-demand alerts.

## Dataset

[UCI Power Consumption of Tetouan City](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/849/power+consumption+of+tetouan+city).

## Current stage

Repository setup and team review of the data and implementation. Source code, experiments and reports will be committed in stages after the team reviews their purpose, assumptions and results.

## Planned scope

- Initial benchmark using classroom methods through chapter 10.
- Later experiments on regime adaptation and a data-quality gate with fallback.
- English notebook, slides and report, with verified member contributions.

Later extensions remain planned. This repository does not yet claim a completed implementation or a proven research contribution.
